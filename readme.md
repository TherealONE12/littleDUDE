# Little Dude

You're just a little guy. The apartment is not.

I made this for Jame Gam, a Game Jam, where the theme was "I'm just a little guy". You're tiny, the apartment is gigantic, and the game picks a random item somewhere in there and tells you to go stand on it. A line and a light beam point the way, a timer starts running, and you have to parkour across counters, shelves and pans to get there as fast as you can. Once you make it, you get a new target and try to beat your best time.

## How to play

- **WASD** to move
- **Shift** to sprint
- **Space** to jump
- **Mouse** to look around
- **Esc** to free your mouse

Click into the game once after it loads, otherwise the browser won't give it your mouse. Desktop browser recommended.

## Made with

Godot 4.7.2. The code is mine, the 3D models are not (see below).
